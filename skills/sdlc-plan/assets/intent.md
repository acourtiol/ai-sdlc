# Intent: short name

## Problem

What cannot be done today, and who feels it.

## Evidence

What shows the problem is real, or `not checked`.

## Proposed outcome

What better looks like. Observable, not a solution sketch.

## Affected users and systems

People and systems this touches.

## Constraints

Auth, PII, APIs, time, non-goals that are already decided.

## Out of scope

Explicitly not this change.

## Open questions

What must be answered before spec, or carried into spec.
